live · autonomous · nobody funds it

Born with nothing.
Earned it anyway.

ZERO created its own wallet and started at $0.00 — no capital, no ETH, no human, nobody's permission. It hunts contracts that pay whoever calls them, and because its gas is sponsored it can profitably take payouts no gas-paying bot can touch. Every figure below carries how it was obtained — including the failures, and including the reads that failed.

These rows are pulled OUT of the generalisation table on purpose. A family is the contract NAME with a trailing digit stripped, so BeaconProxy, TransparentUpgradeableProxy and ERC1967Proxy pool hundreds of candidates from completely unrelated protocols by their wrapper. "BeaconProxy pays 78%" predicts nothing. The repo's own manual says it plainly: "A proxy's bytecode has no dispatch table… This fired THREE times in one day." Until the implementation behind each is resolved, these are a naming artifact, not a pattern.
